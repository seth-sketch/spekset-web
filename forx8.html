A real desk. Just type.

FOR-Control is the box's home screen: an Eos-style desk in the same file as the drawing. The stage in plan or 3D, a fader for every light, a command line, and one cue list for the lights and the screens.

CHAN 1 THRU 8 AT 50 · 3 FULL · 5 OUTLevels, by number. Add TIME 3 and they fade.
RECORD CUE 5 TIME 3 FOLLOW 2Record the stage as a cue, with a time, a follow, a hang and a label.
RECORD CUE 5 LIGHTS · VIDEO · BOTHChoose what the cue carries. A lights cue never touches the screens; a video cue never touches the lights.
GO · BACK · GOTO CUE 5 TIME 0Run the show, step back, or jump to any cue.
BLIND CUE 5 · SNEAK · UPDATEEdit a cue without touching the stage, release hand changes back to the cue, or write the stage into it.
RECORD SUB 3 · LOAD 3 GMSubmasters on top of the cue (highest wins), and load any fader as a sub, the master, an effect rate, the time scale or a crossfader.
EFFECT 6 RATE 50Sixteen effects: pulse, chase, strobe, twinkle, rainbow, colour chase, circle, figure 8, pan and tilt sweeps, ballyhoo and more.
MOVERS · AIMSelect every moving light, then tap the stage in plan or 3D and they point there. The aim is recorded in the cue.

Colour, gels and tint

Hue and saturation, the LEE and Rosco gel books (with "similar" to find the nearest gel), and a tint row: warmer, cooler, saturation and green. Colour temperature in Kelvin for CCT lights.

Encoders that follow you

Pick LEVEL, FOCUS, COLOR or BEAM and the knobs become pan and tilt, zoom, edge or iris for the selected lights.

Three layouts

DESK shows everything. STAGE fills the screen for aiming by touch. CUES gives you the cue list as cards and tall faders. The box remembers your choice.

Patch and RDM

RIG opens the patch: every light's universe and address, auto-patch, and RDM discovery on the network, matched to the fixture library.

Haze, lock and dim

HAZE 70 shows the beams on the stage. LOCK locks the console; DIM 10 dims it after ten idle minutes.

DEMO

Press DEMO and the console runs a show by itself, with a moving cursor and captions: Keynote, Theatre, Band and Show day. Esc stops it and leaves nothing running.

A video switcher, cued with the lights.

Every screen in your show (LED walls, IMAG, TVs and projectors) goes on an output. Each output gets a preview and a program row. Record a cue and the screens go with the lights; a video cue can follow a lights cue.

Preview, then take

CUT, AUTO at your rate, the T-bar by hand, fade to black, or ALL OUTS to take every output at once.

Gang outputs

Gang two IMAG screens and they preview, take and fade together while the main wall stays on its own.

Live sources

Drop in stills and clips. A USB capture card brings in a camera or a laptop as a live source.

1080p or 4K outputs

Set each output's size, its monitor and its fade time. Each screen sits at its place in the output's raster.

Multiview

One window with every output's program and preview, the cue line and the gang tags, on the monitor you choose.

One cue list

GO moves only what a cue carries, so lights, screens or both run from the same list and the same GO.
