Design from what's on the shelf.

Asset Control is for rental houses and venues. Import your inventory and every design draws from it: stock counts in the picker, unstocked products locked, shortages flagged before the quote.

  • ImportA RentalWorks export, or any CSV or Excel sheet with item, quantity and location.
  • ConstrainTurn on "Constrain to my inventory" and designers can only pick products you stock.
  • Shortage reportUsed, available and short while the design is still a draft. It exports to Excel.
  • SwapRight-click a wall to change it to a product you stock. The grid, content and rigging stay put.

Today it covers LED screens. Lighting and the rest of the shop are next.
