The trial and the license

What do I get in the 20 days?

The whole app. Every workspace, every library, the Spec PDF and all 20 exports, unlimited projects. Nothing is held back for paying customers.

Why do you need a card to start?

So the account carries straight through if you keep it. We email you before the first charge. Cancel before day 20 and nothing is charged.

What does it cost after the trial?

$1,800 a year, billed annually. One plan. No monthly option.

Can I cancel?

Yes, from your account page, any time. [tbc] The license runs to the end of the period you paid for. Your show files stay downloadable as .json backups.

Is there a team plan?

Each designer needs a license. A second designer can be a Collaborator on your show without a license of their own for review and comment; they need a license to edit. [tbc] For a studio with several seats, write to us.

What is Web Control and the F App?

Add-ons to the design license for running lights. Web Control ($300 a year) runs lights from the browser through the Forectra Companion with your own sACN node. The F App ($600 a year) is the Mac and PC desktop app; the show runs locally even if the internet drops. The FOR-X 8 console includes its license.

2

The FOR-X 8 console

What is it?

A lighting and video desk in a road case: a 14-inch touch screen, a 5-inch touch panel, eight faders with OLED labels, encoders, GO / BACK / BLACKOUT, eight universes of DMX, four switchable video outputs and camera capture in. It runs the same show file as the app. Sheet 11 of the manual has the hardware detail.

What does $8,495 include?

The console, two years of Forectra license and support, and the road case. After year two the license renews at the design-license price. [tbc]

How long to ship?

About four weeks from order. Each one is built to order. We email a tracking number when it leaves.

Where do you ship?

United States and Canada at launch. Elsewhere, write to us first.

What about warranty?

One year, parts and labour, on the console. The GEEKOM computer inside carries its maker's own warranty on top. Road-case damage from handling is not covered. [tbc — warranty terms to confirm with counsel before the first shipment]

Can I return it?

Within 14 days of delivery, unused and in its case, for a refund less return shipping. After that, warranty applies. [tbc]

Is my data on the box?

Your show files live in your account and in the box's local storage. Anyone with a Forectra account can sign in on the box and load their own shows. A thumb-drive .json works too.

Using Forectra

What does it import?

MVR, DXF, OBJ, GLB and PDF plans. Vectorworks plots come in as MVR or DXF.

What does it export?

Spec PDF, Plan PDF, Sheet Composer plates, SVG floor plan, GLB, OBJ, MVR, disguise CSV, content manifest, Datasmith, nDisplay config, scene CSV and PNG, budget, and more. The manual lists all 20.

Does it control real lights?

Yes, with Web Control, the F App or the FOR-X 8: sACN and Art-Net out, RDM discovery, 2,194 fixture profiles with real DMX modes.

Does it switch real video?

On the FOR-X 8, yes: four switchable outputs, a USB capture card as a live source, preview/program per output, CUT, AUTO, T-bar, fade to black. Lights and video share one cue list.

Does it do audio?

Audio is designed, not controlled: line arrays, point sources and subs go in the model and print on the speaker schedule. Forectra does not run audio playback or mixing.

Which browser?

Chrome or Edge, current version, on a Mac or PC with a real GPU. Safari works for review links. No tablet or phone editing. [tbc]

Can I work offline?

The browser app needs a connection to save. The F App and the FOR-X 8 run the show locally; sync happens when you are back online. [tbc]
