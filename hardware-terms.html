What you are buying

The FOR-X 8 is a lighting and video console in a road case. What it includes, its screens, controls and connections, is listed on the FOR-X 8 product page. That page, as it reads on the day you order, is the description of what you are buying.

Your order includes the console in its road case and two years of the Forectra license and support, as the product page states.

Price and payment

The FOR-X 8 is $8,495 (US dollars), paid once, by card through our payment processor, Stripe, at checkout. We do not hold your card details.

The $8,495 price is for the console. Shipping and sales tax are added at checkout: you enter your delivery address, choose a shipping option, and checkout shows the shipping cost and any sales tax due for that address before you pay (see section 4). Prices on our website can change, but a change never affects an order you have already paid for.

Your order

Each FOR-X 8 is built to order. When you pay, you get a receipt by email and your console goes into the build queue.

If we can't fulfil your order, for example because a part is no longer available or we can't ship to your address, we may cancel it. If we do, we refund everything you paid, in full, to the card you paid with, and tell you why.

  • Risk of loss passes to you when the console is delivered. Until then, if it is lost or damaged in transit, that is our problem, not yours.

Dead on arrival

If your console arrives damaged or doesn't work out of the box, email hello@forectra.com within 7 days of delivery, with your order details and, if you can, a photo of the damage and the box.

We will repair or replace it at our cost, including the shipping both ways. No restocking fee applies.

30-day returns

If the FOR-X 8 isn't right for you, you can return it for a refund within 30 days of delivery, as long as:

  • it is complete, with everything it came with;
  • it is undamaged; and
  • it comes back in its original packaging.

Email hello@forectra.com first and we will send you a return authorisation and the address to ship to. You pay the return shipping, and we recommend you insure it, because the console is your responsibility until it reaches us.

When the console arrives and we have checked it, we refund the price less a 10% restocking fee ($849.50 on an $8,495 console) to the card you paid with. Outbound shipping and any import charges you paid are not refunded.

When a console is returned, the two years of license that came with it end on the date of the refund.
