Privacy Policy

What we keep,
and why.

Draft for counsel review — not yet in force. This policy covers forectra.com, the Forectra app at app.forectra.com, the desktop apps and the FOR-X 8 console. Short version: we keep what we need to run your account and your shows, we don't sell it, and you can take it or delete it.

1

What we collect

Account. Name, email, company, and the password hash. Payment is handled by Stripe; we see the card's last four digits and expiry, never the full number.

Your shows. The design files, drawings, images, guest lists, cue lists and exports you create or upload. These are your content (see the Terms).

Usage. Which features you use, errors, browser and device type, and performance timings, so we can fix what breaks and see what matters. Help → Report a bug sends us the show file and the log, with your consent each time.

Hardware. A FOR-X 8 reports its software version and health to us when it is online, and its DMX/network configuration when you ask for support. It does not send your show unless you sign in.

Website. Server logs (IP address, pages, referrer) and privacy-respecting analytics without cross-site tracking. [tbc — name the analytics provider once chosen]

Email. If you write to us, we keep the correspondence.

2

What we don't do

We don't sell personal data. We don't run advertising networks on the site. We don't read your shows except to support you when you ask, or to investigate abuse of the Service.

3

Providers who run parts of the Service on our behalf, under contract, only for that purpose:

Who What for
Stripe Payments and invoices
Cloudflare Hosting the website and app, DDoS protection, DNS
HubSpot Email to customers and trial users, support correspondence
Google Workspace Our email
Dropbox Our internal file storage

We also disclose data when the law requires, or to protect the Service, our users, or others.

4

Email

Trial and subscription emails (welcome, reminder before a charge, receipts, service notices) are part of the Service. Product news is optional; every such email has an unsubscribe link, and you can also write to us.

5

Keeping it

Account and show data stay while your account is open. After you close the account you can export for 30 days, after which we delete the shows; billing records are kept as long as tax law requires. Backups roll off within 90 days.

6

Your rights

Wherever you are, you can ask us to show you what we hold, correct it, export it, or delete it, by writing to hello@forectra.com. We answer within 30 days. If you are in the EU, UK or California you have the specific rights those laws give you, and we honour them.

7

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest with our providers. Access inside Forectra is limited to the people who need it to run the Service. No system is perfect; if we learn of a breach affecting your data we will tell you promptly.

8

Children

The Service is for professionals and is not directed at anyone under 18.

9

Changes

We will post changes here and, for material changes, email account holders at least 14 days before they take effect.
